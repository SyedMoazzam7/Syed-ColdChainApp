About FrostLine

FrostLine is a temperature-controlled logistics company based in Vadodara. We move and store goods that spoil or lose potency when they warm up, and we monitor temperature from pickup to delivery.

Target customers
Pharma distributors, dairies, seafood exporters, hospitals, restaurant chains and ice-cream makers.
Service area
Gujarat and Maharashtra, with partner lanes to Delhi NCR and Bengaluru.
Value proposition
Lower spoilage, a temperature record for every delivery, and an alert before a load goes out of range.

Industry reference: National Centre for Cold-chain Development (cold chain standards and policy) and FSSAI (food safety rules our food customers must meet).
